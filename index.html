What we take on

From an empty plot to the keys in your hand.

Turnkey construction

One contract covering drawings, grey structure, finishing and fittings. You deal with us, not with fifteen separate contractors.

Grey structure

Foundation, columns, slabs, blockwork and roofing — built to the structural drawings, with steel and concrete you can inspect at any stage.

Finishing & interiors

Plaster, flooring, joinery, electrical and plumbing, paint and fixtures. Taking over a half-finished structure is fine too.

Commercial & institutional

Schools, hotels and shop-cum-office buildings, where phasing, access and handover dates matter as much as the build itself.

How a project runs

You always know what stage you're paying for.

01

Site visit

We walk the plot, check access, soil and services, and talk budget honestly before anything is drawn.

02

Drawings & BOQ

Architectural and structural drawings with a line-by-line bill of quantities. You see the cost of every item.

03

Construction

Our own site team and supervisor, weekly photo updates, and payments released against completed stages.

04

Handover

Snag list closed, completion documents in order, and the building handed over clean.
