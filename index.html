About RANA COTTON

Built Around Cotton & Quality

RANA COTTON GINNERS & OIL MILLS (PVT.) LTD. operates in the cotton and oil-mill sector, supporting the journey of cotton from raw agricultural material to carefully processed products.

Our approach is centered on responsible handling, consistent processing, quality awareness and dependable relationships with growers, buyers and business partners.

Our Operations

🌿 Cotton Procurement

Careful sourcing and handling of raw cotton with attention to material quality and reliable supply relationships.

⚙ Cotton Ginning

Ginning operations designed to separate cotton lint from seed while maintaining careful processing and material handling.

🛢 Oil Mill Operations

Oil-mill operations connected with cottonseed processing, supporting efficient utilization of cottonseed as part of the value chain.
